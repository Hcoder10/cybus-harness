# Audio design

Covers music, sound effects, UI sounds, ambience, and the mix. Audio cannot be seen in captures: the judge
reviews an AUDIO MANIFEST (every Sound: id, loaded length, volume, group, looped, 3D or 2D, what triggers
it) and the trigger map from the code, plus whatever recording is available.

## Capture
- The audio manifest (generated by walking the DataModel and the sound module), with each asset's load
  result (loaded, TimeLength > 0).
- A coverage table: every player input, UI interaction, reward, failure, state change, and area -> the sound
  it plays.

## Anchors
- **1-2**: silence, or sound ids that fail to load; one sound for everything.
- **4**: a few stock sounds and one looping track; no UI sounds; unbalanced volumes; music restarts on
  respawn.
- **7**: full coverage (every input, UI interaction, reward, failure has a sound), sound groups for music /
  sfx / ui / ambience with a sane mix, pitch and volume variation on repeated sounds, music that changes
  with the game state, ambience per area, 3D sounds positioned in the world.
- **9**: audio that carries the feel: layered hits (transient + body + tail), dynamic music (intensity layers
  or stingers on payoffs), ducking under key moments, a recognisable sonic identity, and silence used on
  purpose.

## Checks
1. **Coverage.** No silent verbs, buttons, rewards or failures.
2. **Validity.** Every asset loads (TimeLength > 0); no copyrighted music; only assets available to the
   game.
3. **Mix.** Separate groups with volumes; UI quieter than impacts; music under gameplay; nothing clipping;
   no ear fatigue from a loud sound repeated every second.
4. **Variation.** Repeated sounds randomise pitch (+-5-10%) and/or pick from a small set.
5. **Space.** Gameplay sounds are 3D on parts with sensible roll-off; UI and music are 2D.
6. **Music states.** Menu / explore / tension / reward music or layers, crossfaded, never hard-cut, never
   restarting on respawn.
7. **Ducking.** Music and ambience dip under payoffs and dialogue.
8. **Settings.** Music and sound volume sliders or toggles that persist.

## Common failures -> fixes
- Silent UI -> hover tick, press click, confirm and back sounds from one family.
- Same sound 50 times a minute -> pitch variation, a round-robin set, a cooldown.
- One track forever -> at least two states with a crossfade and a stinger on the signature moment.
